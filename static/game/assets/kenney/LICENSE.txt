Tiles and sprites in this directory are built from two Kenney asset packs
by Kenney Vleugels (www.kenney.nl):

1. "Roguelike/RPG Pack" (https://kenney.nl/assets/roguelike-rpg-pack)
   - tree_*.png, bush_round.png, flower_*.png, ground_*.png
   - with help by Lynn Evers

2. "Monster Builder Pack" (https://kenney.nl/assets/monster-builder-pack)
   - if_golem.png, else_wraith.png, boss_exception.png
   - these are hand-composited from that pack's modular body/eye/mouth/
     detail parts (not shipped as pre-made monsters)
